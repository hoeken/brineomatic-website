# Sensors and Actuators

Brineomatic is designed to be compatible with a variety of different sensors and actuators.

## Sensors

### Pressure Sensors (4–20 mA)

Brineomatic supports standard 4–20 mA analog pressure sensors for monitoring filter inlet and membrane pressure.

### TDS / Salinity Sensors

Two TDS (Total Dissolved Solids) sensor inputs are provided for measuring product water and brine salinity.

### Flow Meters (Pulse)

Two pulse-type flow meter inputs are supported for measuring product and brine flow rates.

### Temperature Sensors (DS18B20)

Two DS18B20 digital temperature sensor inputs are available for monitoring motor and water temperature.

## Actuators

### Relays / Solenoid Drivers

Brineomatic provides 5 relay/solenoid driver outputs (3A max each) for controlling solenoid valves and other loads.

### Stepper Motor Driver

One onboard stepper motor driver is available for controlling a motorized valve or similar actuator.

### Servo Connectors

Two 5V servo connectors are provided for standard RC-style servo actuators.

### VFD / RS485 (Optional)

Optional RS485 Modbus support allows control of Variable Frequency Drive (VFD) pump controllers such as the GD20.
